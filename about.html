About

From the stage to the screen.

I came to interaction design through cognitive science and theater. Both are about how people perceive, feel and act.

I’m a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon’s Entertainment Technology Center, producing and designing experiences for new platforms. Before that I studied Cognitive Science (Design & Interaction) at UC San Diego.

It started in a small college theater, where our team rebuilt a play around light and sound because the stage couldn’t do what the script asked. I still design that way: start from what the space and the audience can do.
