About

From the stage to the screen

I came to interaction design through cognitive science and theater. Both are about the same thing: understanding how people perceive, feel and act — and shaping an experience around them.

Hi, I’m Oulan

I’m a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon’s Entertainment Technology Center, where I produce and design interactive experiences for new platforms — most recently a 360° cylindrical display and Tilt Five tabletop AR.

Before ETC, I studied Cognitive Science with a specialization in Design & Interaction at UC San Diego, with minors in Design and Japanese Studies. There I worked on HCI research, social computing and UX projects, and spent several quarters on teaching teams.

What draws me to this work started in a small college theater. Adapting a folk tale for a stage that couldn’t handle the scene changes the script asked for, our team rebuilt the story around light, sound and where the actors stood — and a blue wash turned a bare set into a moonlit river. I still design that way: start from what the space and the audience can actually do, then make that the experience.
