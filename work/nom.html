Problem

Too many options, no decision.

Faced with long lists, students got frustrated or abandoned the search.

How might we help students decide where to eat quickly, without losing what makes it personal?

Solution

Swipe to decide.

01

Swipe recommendations

Quick, visual picks that learn your taste.

02

Gallery home

Nearby spots, student reviews and discounts.

03

Map search

Filters for price and aesthetics.

Iteration

Giving users control over “a good match.”

Testing showed some students chose on price, others on looks. In round two I added aesthetic filters alongside price — personal, without bringing back the long list.

Reflection

Familiar patterns lower the learning curve.

Borrowing the swipe from social apps made a new kind of search feel instantly usable.
