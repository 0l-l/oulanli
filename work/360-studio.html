360 Studio

Producer & UX Design

In progress · Fall 2026

A circular display, with one rotating controller that everyone shares. What does play look like when players stand around the screen instead of in front of it? At Carnegie Mellon’s Entertainment Technology Center, our team is finding out one prototype and playtest at a time.

My part

  1. ProducerKeep the team moving: communication with people outside the team, planning and documentation, and the playtest and interview process.
  2. UX DesignResearch how people play around the screen, and turn what we see in playtests into design changes.
