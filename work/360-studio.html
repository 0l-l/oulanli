360 Studio

Producer & UX Design

In progress · Fall 2026

360 Studio is an exploratory project at Carnegie Mellon University’s Entertainment Technology Center investigating how a 360° cylindrical screen can create shared, in-person play. Through multiplayer game prototypes, custom physical interfaces, and iterative playtesting, we explore how the screen’s wraparound form shapes player movement, visibility, communication, and collaboration. Our final deliverables include playable experiences and a design handbook documenting the device’s capabilities, limitations, and design considerations.

My part

  1. ProducerKeep the team moving: communication with people outside the team, planning and documentation, and the playtest and interview process.
  2. UX DesignResearch how people play around the screen, and turn what we see in playtests into design changes.
