Problem

Finding ten seconds in a fifty-minute lecture.

Recorded lectures have no structure, no summaries and weak search. Review becomes scrubbing.

How might we turn passive lecture video into a space learners can explore?

Process

Three ways to map a lecture.

Version 1

Spider diagram

Structure became visible, but the web of links grew hard to read.

Version 2

Grouped nodes + double timeline

Showed both where you are and how ideas connect.

Final

Layered learning

Topic → subtopic → detail, with AI summaries, notes and keyword search.

My work

Segmentation, prototypes, roadmap.

I built the flexible video segmentation interface, prototyped the spider diagram, search and note-taking views, and outlined the path to a full AI-powered platform.

Next

Plan the evidence early.

We ended with an evaluation plan — a 10–20 person study with objective and survey measures — because a learning tool has to prove it helps people learn.
