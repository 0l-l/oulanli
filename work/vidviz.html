VidViz

Lecture recordings are long, linear and hard to review. VidViz is a visual learning space, powered by generative AI, that turns a long video into a structure you can explore.

My role

Timeline

Problem

A mismatch between lectures and learning

Finding one ten-second explanation can mean scrubbing through fifty minutes of video. Recorded lectures have limited search, no structure and no summaries. They ask for passive listening at a fixed pace, while effective learning is active, exploratory and adapted to the learner.

My work

Prototyping new ways into a video

  • Flexible video segmentation — an interface for building hierarchical visual maps of a lecture, so learners can jump between topics instead of scrubbing.
  • Interactive prototypes — spider diagrams, advanced keyword search and a note-taking system that connects notes to moments in the video.
  • Roadmap — next steps toward a full platform, with AI automating segmentation and personalizing content.
