Overview

A visual learning space for long-form video

VidViz is a research prototype from Prof. Jim Hollan’s course on how computing can augment human thinking. It uses generative AI to structure a long lecture into a hierarchy learners can explore.

Timeline
10 weeks · Spring 2024
My role
HCI Researcher
Team
Team Synergy — Xiaolin Wu, Oulan Li, Matthew Garcia, Esther Tae
Responsibilities
Video segmentation interface · Interactive prototypes · Future roadmap

Solution

Layered learning

01

A hierarchy of the lecture

The video is segmented into main topic, subtopics and detail, so learners jump between ideas instead of scrubbing.

02

AI that explains

A language model generates a summary, explanation and example for each segment.

03

Notes and keyword search

Notes link to moments in the video, and search finds the ten seconds you need in a fifty-minute lecture.

Problem

A mismatch between lectures and learning

Lecture recordings ask for passive listening at a fixed pace. Effective learning is active, exploratory and adapted to the learner. Today students cope by taking notes and guessing timestamps, or borrowing a classmate’s notes — neither makes review effective.

Gap 01

Limited search

Finding one explanation can mean scrubbing through the whole video.

Gap 02

No structure

Recordings have no organization beyond a timeline.

Gap 03

No summaries

Key points are never extracted for review.

HOW MIGHT WEturn passive lecture videos into a space learners can actively explore?

Process

Two renditions before the final interface

Version 1

Spider diagram

Topics as nodes connected by lines, with AI-generated connections shown separately.

Takeaway

Structure became visible, but the web of links grew hard to read for a full lecture.

Version 2

Grouped nodes and a double timeline

Nodes grouped by their relationships, paired with a timeline view and a graph view.

Takeaway

Learners need both “where am I in the lecture” and “how do these ideas connect” — the final version layers them.

Next steps

From prototype to evidence

We closed the quarter with an evaluation plan rather than claims: an experiment with 10–20 learners comparing objective results with and without VidViz, plus surveys and ratings for the subjective experience. The roadmap then moves to a coded implementation with more accurate automatic segmentation.

Reflection

  1. Structure is the interface

    Each version changed how the lecture was organized, not just how it looked — and that changed what learners could do.

  2. Plan the evidence early

    A tool for learning has to prove it helps people learn; designing the evaluation is part of designing the product.
